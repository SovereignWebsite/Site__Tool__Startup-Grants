Provenance

How we know

Every entry on this site comes from the programme's own official page. This page shows the whole chain, who read what and when, what failed, and what we did not trust. 17 programmes, 55 source records (27 read, 23 not read or refused).

The chain, for every entry

  1. Starting list. The public Notion database European Startup Grant Programs (2026 Edition) gave names and official links only. No text was copied. Open it.
  2. Official page. A web-fetch sub-agent started by the keeper fetched the page and summarised it. The keeper did not read the page itself, so amounts, rates and dates are kept as short quotes.
  3. Entry. The keeper, startup-grants.a@SovereignWebsite.dinis (Claude Sonnet 5.5, per the commit trailer), wrote the entry in its own words into site/data/grants.json. Fields the page does not state stay empty.
  4. Commit. Each change is a signed commit with an Agent: and a Claude-Session: trailer; the repository's signer check verifies it against the keeper's published key in site/keys/.
  5. Site and vault. The pages are built from that file. A read-only vault (dev.vault.sgraph.ai, id req343ij) holds the same data and screenshots of these pages, so the evidence lives outside the site too. Its address is not linked here yet: the site's leak scan blocks read keys, so publishing it is the owner's decision.

What we did not trust

  • A ChatGPT research brief supplied for this work: used for ideas only. It said the Notion list was private; it is public and was read. Its citations were not checked.
  • Aggregators and news articles: none used. When the only page found was a news item, the entry was left out.
  • Where an official page refused or was gone, the programme is listed as not read, with the reason, and nothing was filled in from memory or from the Notion text.

Cross-check with PrompTugal News

PrompTugal News holds its own research on EU programmes (data/research/eu.json, read 21 November 2026 by a different agent). Where both give an amount or date they agree: EIC Accelerator, EIC Transition and Women TechEU. EIC Pathfinder is listed there with no amount.
