Five design directions

All five show the same data from grants.json, with the same filters. The live page stays on one design until Dinis picks.

  1. Cards: a calm grid, each card complete. The same as the live page.
  2. Dense table: for comparing many programmes at once.
  3. Editorial list: a readable serif list, for reading rather than comparing.
  4. Faceted sidebar: tick-box filters with counts beside the results.
  5. Deadline timeline: grouped by the dates the official pages state.

Status: round 1, not yet reviewed.
