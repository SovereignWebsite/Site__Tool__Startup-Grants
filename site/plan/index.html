Plan

The map: sources, provenance, refresh and the fit check

This page maps what exists, what is next, and what needs a decision. It is a plan, not a promise: each step says who does it and what it needs.

What exists now

  • Data: 17 programmes and 25 open UK competitions from official pages, 20 starting-list items and other addresses not yet read, with reasons (grants.json).
  • Provenance: a record for every entry and every address tried (Provenance, sources.json), in the same shape PrompTugal News uses for its own facts.
  • Semantic graph: funders, programmes, countries, stages, sectors, official pages and readers as nodes and edges (Graph, graph.json, grants.jsonld).
  • Fit check: a form that compares a startup's answers with the criteria the official pages state (Check my fit).
  • Vault: the data, the provenance and screenshots of these pages, kept in a read-only vault outside the site. It exists and renders; its address is not linked here until the owner decides how to publish a read key, because the site's leak scan blocks read keys.

Overlap with PrompTugal News

PrompTugal News Startups has 577 facts about Portuguese startups, incubators, investors and rounds, 17 of them EU programmes. Ours is programme-first and Europe-wide. They agree where they overlap. Proposed: PrompTugal reads grants.json for programmes; this site reads PrompTugal's place pages for local incubators, which are out of scope here.

Refresh: how the site keeps itself current

Facts are never changed by a script. A refresh only finds out what may have changed and asks an agent or a person to check.

  1. Fetch: a scheduled job reads every address in sources.json and stores a hash of the page text and the date.
  2. Compare: a different hash, a passed deadline, or an address that now fails raises a flag.
  3. Report: flags go into a short report, one line per programme, with the old and new text side by side.
  4. Re-read: the keeper (or a person) re-reads the flagged pages and updates the entry and its date_checked, in a signed commit.
  5. Publish: the site rebuilds; the vault gets a new snapshot, so every version stays on record.

Needs a decision: where the job runs. It cannot be on this static site. Options are a scheduled GitHub Actions job in this repository, or the keeper's own scheduled session. Either needs the permission to read the blocked pages in a browser to be settled first.

Where it is not enough

  • 20 pages refused automated reading, have moved or are hubs; they stay gaps until read.
  • The fit check can only use criteria the pages state in numbers or places. Everything else is shown as text for the reader to judge.
  • Nothing here is advice, and only the funder decides who is eligible.
